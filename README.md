# 💫 About Me  

Hi, I’m Nathan 👋  

I work with data to solve problems, improve operations, and help teams make better decisions. My background is in fintech operations, customer experience, risk, and recovery, with over 10 years of experience leading teams and improving processes in fast-paced environments.  

What draws me to data is the ability to turn complex operational problems into clear insights and actionable decisions. I enjoy translating data into something practical and understandable for non-technical teams, combining operational experience with an analytical mindset to improve workflows, reporting, and customer outcomes.  

Today, I’m continuing to build deeper expertise in analytics engineering, SQL, dbt, and BI development while working on projects focused on automation, reporting, and data modeling.

## 🔑 Quick Hits

- 🗄️ SQL (BigQuery, Snowflake, PostgreSQL, MySQL)  
- 🛠️ dbt for data modeling & transformation  
- 📊 BI tools: Sigma, Looker, Tableau, Sisense, Metabase  
- 🤖 Workflow automation using Claude, GPT, Gemini, Zapier & n8n  
- 📈 Built KPI dashboards and operational reporting for leadership ([View Sigma Dashboard](https://public.sigmacomputing.com/view-workbook?workbook=3QbmQ9i2SS8W9vYr6qjqqb))  
- 🚀 Used data to reduce manual work, improve workflows, and support better customer outcomes
